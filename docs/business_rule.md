# Business Rules and Operational Dynamics

## 1. Customer Registration and Profiles
- **Agility Focus:** Registration must collect only essentials (name, CPF/Tax ID, email, phone, date of birth) to avoid friction.
- **Quick Identification:** Use of a `registration_number` for fast lookups at the front desk.
- **Behavioral Profile:** The system allows tagging player profiles (e.g., troublemaker, casual) and managing complaint/report histories.
- **Walk-in Players:** During high-traffic moments, the system must allow checking in players without a full profile (anonymous/walk-ins).
- **Consent Term:** Requirement for digital acceptance, differentiating open-game and private-game participants for remarketing purposes.

## 2. Game Types and Check-in
- **Open Game:** High rotation, individual billing.
- **Private Game:** Group-focused. Incentive rule: organizers who guarantee 10 upfront paying players receive a fee waiver for the game.
- **Participation Modalities:** Strict price differentiation for **Equipped Players** (owning their own gear) versus **Rental Players** (using field armory kits).

## 3. Cash Register and Tabs (Snack Bar / Armory)
- **Refills:** Quick logging of consumables/refills (BBs, gas) linked to rentals.
- **Flexible Payment:** Option to pay for items instantly or leave them open on the player's tab for unified settlement at the end of the event.

## 4. Financial Management, Charts, and AI Projections
- The system consolidates revenues by game, day, month, and year.
- **Seasonality:** Comparison charts must focus on matching the same month from previous years to bypass variables like holidays and weather.
- **AI Projections:** The growth projection module (AI) must only process and forecast after accumulating at least 3 solid months of historical data.
